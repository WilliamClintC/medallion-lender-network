    Read with care

    • No dollar amounts. Every number is a count of medallions (fractional where a loan is shared), not loan dollars. UCC filings record no loan principal.
    • Holder of record: a sale is dated by its assignment filing.
    • A sale can go unfiled, so some moves are missing or late.
    • Exits are mostly lapses, dated up to 5 years after payoff.
    • Public = medallions with no open lien on record (loan closed, lapsed, or never filed); not proof the owner is debt-free.
    • Moves among the small lenders inside "Other lenders" are not drawn (count per year in the inspector).
    • Persistence = share of the start-of-year book still with the same holder at year end; moves within "Other lenders" count as staying.
    • Persistence ring: arc from 12 o'clock covering that share; a full ring = kept its whole book.
    • Persistence rings stay year-specific in every window, including Memory and Since 2000.
    • Memory: each year's moves count half as much after every half-life (default 1 year), so recent moves are drawn strongest.
    • Memory colour strength = share of the decayed total that comes from this year and last year; old moves fade to a pale tint.
    • Memory share = decayed moves ÷ the sender's decayed start-of-year books (same half-life).
    • Timeline dates come from the project registries (_timeline.py, stages 229 and 232); stage 232 wins where they differ. MRP dates and the 2023 PenFed re-filing are from the project timeline notes.
    • Markov share = medallions moved ÷ sender's book at the start of the year.
    • "Since 2000" pools the share: total moved ÷ sum of the sender's start-of-year books.
    • Arrow width grows with the square root of the flow, so small flows stay visible. Read exact counts in the inspector.
    • All lenders: every family that moved 50 or more medallions over 2000 to 2025; the rest sit in "Other lenders".

    Source: NY DOS UCC filings via Lexis. Holders and moves from stage 251 (holder of record, latest-loan rule); networks from stage 255 (holder_network.json, holder_network_full.json); page from stage 257.
